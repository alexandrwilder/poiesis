# The name and the mark

The code is free under its licences. The name "Poiesis" and the Poiesis mark are not part of
those licences; they belong to Alexander Adolfsson.

You may, without asking:
- say that your work is built on Poiesis, works with Poiesis, or is a fork of it;
- use the name in articles, talks, reviews and comparisons;
- use the mark unchanged to point at the official project.

Please ask first before you:
- call a fork, product or service "Poiesis", or anything easily confused with it;
- use the mark as your own logo or app icon;
- present your builds, updates or services as official Poiesis ones.

A fork of the app gets its own name and icon. When in doubt, open an issue and ask.
